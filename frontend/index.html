How The System Works

Simple 3-step pipeline combining computer vision with clinical guidance.

1. Image Upload

Upload Left, Right, or Both eye photos via drag-and-drop dropzones with automatic 224×224 normalization.

2. TensorFlow CNN AI Model

Deep neural network evaluates image features against trained dataset to calculate disease confidence scores.

3. Insights & Nearby Care

View condition analysis, preventative tips, discuss with Gemini AI chatbot, and locate nearby ophthalmologists via Google Maps.

Supported Eye Conditions

AI preliminary screening recognizes six major ophthalmic conditions.

Moderate Risk
Cataract

Lens clouding that impairs vision, creates glare sensitivity, and dims color brightness.

High Risk
Glaucoma

Optic nerve damage associated with elevated intraocular pressure, leading to peripheral vision loss.

High Risk
Diabetic Retinopathy

Retinal microvascular damage resulting from systemic hyperglycemia and micro-aneurysms.

Moderate Risk
AMD (Macular Degeneration)

Deterioration of central macular vision affecting reading acuity and straight-line perception.

Mild / Acute
Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)

Inflammation of the outer ocular membrane causing redness, itchiness, and discharge.

Healthy
Normal Healthy Eye

No significant structural anomalies or pathological lesions observed in preliminary screening.
